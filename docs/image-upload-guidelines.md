# Image Upload Guidelines

## Store Logo Requirements

### Recommended Specifications

- **Size**: 200x200px (minimum) to 1000x1000px (maximum)
- **Format**: PNG, JPG, or WebP
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square)
- **File Size**: Under 5MB

### Design Tips for Best Results

#### Logo Composition

- **Center your logo**: Place your main logo/text in the center of a square canvas
- **Add padding**: Leave some white space around your logo so it doesn't get cut off when displayed as a circle
- **High contrast**: Use colors that contrast well with both light and dark backgrounds

#### Common Issues and Solutions

❌ **Problem**: Logo appears cut off or cropped
✅ **Solution**: Ensure your logo has adequate padding (at least 10-20% of the image size) on all sides

❌ **Problem**: Logo looks blurry or pixelated
✅ **Solution**: Use high-resolution images (at least 200x200px, preferably 400x400px or larger)

❌ **Problem**: Logo doesn't work on different backgrounds
✅ **Solution**: Use a logo with a transparent background (PNG) or add a subtle background color

### How Your Logo Will Appear

Your logo will be displayed as a **circular image** in these locations:

- Store settings preview (48x48px circle)
- Storefront header (64x80px circle on mobile/desktop)
- Social media shares and links

### Quick Logo Creation Tips

If you don't have a professional logo:

1. **Use Canva or similar tools**:

   - Create a 400x400px square canvas
   - Add your business name in a nice font
   - Center it with adequate padding
   - Export as PNG

2. **Use your business initials**:

   - Simple text on a colored background
   - Keep it clean and readable

3. **Icon + text combination**:
   - Small icon above or beside your business name
   - Ensure both fit comfortably in the circular frame

### Image Optimization

- **Compress images**: Use tools like TinyPNG to reduce file size
- **Use web formats**: WebP provides better compression than JPG/PNG
- **Test different sizes**: Make sure your logo is readable at small sizes

## Store Banner Requirements

### Recommended Specifications

- **Size**: 1200x400px (3:1 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: PNG, JPG, or WebP
- **File Size**: Under 5MB

### Design Tips

- **Keep text readable**: Use large fonts and high contrast
- **Focus on the center**: Important elements should be in the center third
- **Mobile consideration**: Test how it looks on narrow screens

Your banner will be displayed at the top of your store page and should complement your logo and overall brand design.
